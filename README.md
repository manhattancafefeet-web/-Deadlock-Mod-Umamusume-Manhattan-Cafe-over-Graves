# Manhattan Cafe over Graves (Deadlock)

Replaces Graves with Manhattan Cafe from *Umamusume: Pretty Derby*, with Agnes Tachyon chibis as her minions.

## Install
Download the `.vpk` from [Releases](../../releases) and install it with your Deadlock mod manager (or animations will be broken).

-Replaced UI
Think I got all the Icons used replaced character art
She has 3 different faces for Neutral, Damaged, and Gloating (they do not show in model just art) (maybe i can do a different version with different faces)

-Replaced abilities

1. Jar throw is her Coffee Mug and made it so mug is always there (graves holding her coat animation).
2. The hands from grasp become Hesitant Late Surgers Debuff and the totems become 
Matikanefukukitaru's orb.
4. I cut the race starting gates into 1 gate and the Shamblers with a Chibi Agnes.

-Weapon
Replaced the zombie are with Agnes sleeve its kinda like 1 finger so may act weird

-Voices/Sound Effects
Most voices/sound effects are changed and all grabbed from the game 


All models are in-game assets used UmaViewer to grab all the models 

Weights and bones are a mix of her OG ones and the ones from graves old model
